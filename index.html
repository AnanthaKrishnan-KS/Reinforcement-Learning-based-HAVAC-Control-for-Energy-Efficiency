About this project

  • Problem: each 15 minutes choose OFF, HEAT or COOL for one office zone with solar gain and daytime occupancy.
  • State: (temperature error bin, outside-temperature bin, 3-hour time block) — 13 × 8 × 8 = 832 states.
  • Reward: −[(T − 23)² + 0.5 × energy], so comfort and energy use trade off.
  • Training: tabular Q-learning, α = 0.15, γ = 0.95, ε-greedy decaying from 1.0, 400 episodes.
  • Caveat: the environment is a simplified synthetic model, not a real building. Results show that the RL formulation works, not real-world energy savings.
